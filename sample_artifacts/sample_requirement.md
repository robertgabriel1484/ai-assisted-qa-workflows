# Sample Requirement

As an accounts payable user, I want invoices imported from a source system to appear in the payment dashboard with the correct vendor, amount, tax, status, and dispute information so that finance users can review and process payments accurately.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Imported invoices must display vendor ID, invoice amount, tax amount, gross amount, and status.
- Paid invoices must show a final status of PAID.
- Disputed invoices must show a final status of DISPUTED.
- Open invoices must show a final status of OPEN.
- Dashboard totals must match backend invoice totals.
